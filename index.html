DECK

A personal wall dashboard for one household.

Deck runs on a single computer at home and shows local weather, the 3D printer, the home network, sports scores and similar information on a screen in the house. It is not a public service: there are no user accounts and nobody outside the household can sign in to it.

One of its panels lists new videos from the owner's own YouTube subscriptions. For that, the owner signs in once with Google and grants read-only access to their YouTube account. Deck never posts, comments, likes, subscribes or changes anything on YouTube.
